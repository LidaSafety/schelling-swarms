hackathon repository unchanged. Same runs and same prediction from pairs as `law_points.md`; the totals over all four
rounds reproduce it exactly. Fixed prompt, 2 answers per copy, rule in force, each model alone.

round 1 (late holders). The prediction of a half is the prediction from pairs over that half's chances only.

resamples the swarm's games (200 draws each).
